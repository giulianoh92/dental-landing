Dental Clinic for Odoo 19

A dental practice management module for Odoo 19, bridging clinic and back-office. POMR-compliant clinical records, SOAP consultations, treatment plans and an interactive OWL odontogram — built on standard Odoo apps.

Everything your clinic needs

A complete clinical and operational toolkit, designed around real dental workflows and built on Odoo's native stack.

Patient management with anamnesis

Comprehensive intake with 20+ medical questions, allergies, chronic conditions and surgical history.

Treatment plans with SOAP notes

POMR-compliant clinical records with Subjective, Objective, Assessment and Plan structure.

Interactive odontogram

Custom OWL component with full FDI notation, surface conditions and per-tooth history tracking.

Front desk queue & waiting room

Check patients in, call them from the day's queue and show the turn on a public waiting room screen.

ICD-10 diagnosis coding

Standardized diagnosis catalog with full ICD-10 codes ready for insurance and reporting workflows.

Prescriptions & certificates

Generate sequentially numbered prescriptions and medical certificates with branded PDF reports.

Patient portal with online booking

Secure patient access, digital consents, consultation history and integrated online booking calendar.

Multi-company aware

Run multiple clinic locations under one Odoo instance with proper data isolation per company.

Built on standard Odoo

Built on standard Odoo apps: ORM, OWL components and the mail engine. Optional SMS reminders use Odoo's paid SMS service.
