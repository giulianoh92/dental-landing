How to invoice dental consultations and treatment plans in Odoo 19

Billing closes the loop in a dental practice: the visit happened, the plan moved forward, and now the work has to be invoiced. Dental Clinic - Invoicing (gh_dental_clinic_account) connects Dental Clinic to the standard Odoo 19 invoicing application, so draft customer invoices come directly from appointments, consultations and treatment plans.

Requirements

  • The gh_dental_clinic module, version 19.0.19.0.0 or later.
  • The Odoo account module.
  • At least one sales journal in the company that will invoice.
  • Optional but recommended: a service product linked to each procedure in the catalog.

The bridge is optional. Without it, the clinical workflow keeps working.

Who can invoice

Dental roles do not grant billing rights by themselves. Invoicing follows the standard Odoo billing group, which an administrator assigns to whoever should bill, for example a receptionist. That person can invoice a consultation from the finished appointment without access to clinical content: the wizard only reads billable data such as the procedure, the tooth and the price.

Invoicing an appointment or a consultation

A finished appointment shows a billing state, either To Invoice or Invoiced, so reception can see what is pending. The consultation fee is defined on the appointment type in the configuration, and it can be linked to a product so the invoice line gets the right taxes and income account. You can also invoice from the consultation form itself.

Invoicing a treatment plan

  1. Move the plan to a billable state: approved, in progress or completed.
  2. Mark plan lines as completed as visits happen. Only completed lines that are not yet invoiced are eligible.
  3. Click "Create Invoice" in the plan header. From the patient record there is also an "Invoice Treatment Plan" wizard that only exposes billable data.
  4. The wizard pre-selects the completed, uninvoiced lines. Adjust the selection line by line, or filter by session number to invoice only certain visits.
  5. Review the untaxed amount, taxes and total, computed the same way as the resulting invoice, and confirm.

The result is a draft customer invoice with the patient as the customer, the sales journal, the fiscal position mapped from the patient, and one invoice line per plan line, keeping the procedure name, tooth and surface. You then validate it from Odoo Invoicing like any other invoice.

Taxes and products

Each procedure can point to a service product, which supplies taxes and the income account. If a procedure has no product, the line is still created, but without taxes and with the journal's default income account.

Tracking what has been invoiced

The treatment plan shows an invoicing status: Not Invoiced, Partially Invoiced or Fully Invoiced, together with the amount invoiced and the amount still to invoice. Smart buttons link the plan to its invoices and each invoice back to its plan. The status reflects invoicing, not payment: collection and reconciliation stay in Odoo Invoicing.

Safeguards against mistakes

  • An invoiced line cannot be selected again. If the invoice is cancelled, the line becomes available again.
  • If two users try to invoice the same lines at once, the lines are locked and the second user is asked to retry in a few seconds, so no duplicate invoice is created.
  • A plan with non-cancelled invoices cannot be deleted until those invoices are cancelled or removed.
  • With several companies, the sales journal is resolved in the current company and the invoice is created in the company of the record.

Frequently asked questions

Can one treatment plan produce several invoices?

Yes. Each run of the wizard only considers completed lines that are not yet on a live invoice, so you can invoice session by session. A line that is already invoiced cannot be selected again unless its invoice is cancelled.

What happens if a procedure has no product?

The invoice line is still created with the procedure name (plus tooth and surface) and the price, but without taxes, using the journal's default income account. Link a service product to the procedures you invoice most to get taxes and accounts automatically.

Does the plan show whether the patient has paid?

No. The plan shows its invoicing status and the amounts invoiced and still to invoice. Payments and reconciliation are handled in Odoo Invoicing like any other invoice.
