A dental clinic module for Odoo 19: what it covers

Many dental practices already run Odoo for accounting, sales or purchasing, and still keep their schedule and clinical charts in a separate dental program. Dental Clinic is a module for Odoo 19 that brings the practice itself into the same database: scheduling, patient records, SOAP consultations, an interactive odontogram, treatment plans, a patient portal and, with an optional second module, invoicing.

Why run the practice inside Odoo

Every extra program means duplicated patients, manual reconciliation and one more user list to maintain. Dental Clinic is built on standard Odoo apps instead: a patient is an Odoo contact, appointments use the Odoo calendar, doctors are linked to Odoo employees, and messages go through the standard mail system. The module depends on Contacts, Calendar, Employees, Discuss (mail), Portal and Website, and it does not replace your accounting.

What the clinical module includes

The main module, gh_dental_clinic, covers daily work for reception and dentists:

  • Appointments: a full lifecycle (draft, confirmed, in waiting room, calling, in consultation, done, cancelled, no show), a multi-doctor calendar, and rescheduling with a recorded reason and history.
  • Patient records: problem-oriented medical records with allergies, chronic conditions, medications and surgical history.
  • SOAP consultations: Subjective, Objective, Assessment and Plan notes with ICD-10 diagnoses and a procedure catalog. A signed consultation is final; corrections are recorded as addenda with a mandatory reason.
  • FDI odontogram: 32 adult and 20 deciduous teeth with statuses, surface conditions and a change history. See the odontogram guide.
  • Treatment plans: procedure lines, session tracking, an approval workflow and templates for frequent treatments.
  • Prescriptions, consents and studies: sequentially numbered prescriptions with PDF reports, digital consent forms with signature capture, radiological studies linked to the record, and an implant registry with osseointegration follow-up.
  • Dashboards: a doctor dashboard and a front-desk queue for the day.

Front desk and patient-facing features

  • Patient portal: patients can book online, view appointments, sign consents and access their own records through the Odoo website.
  • Waiting room display: a screen for a TV that shows the current queue and patient status.
  • Recall system: follow-up reminders based on configurable intervals.
  • Notifications: mail templates for confirmations, reminders and recall alerts, plus optional SMS reminders through Odoo's SMS service.

Roles and multi-company

Access is split into two independent privileges: Front Office (receptionist, front office manager) and Clinical (clinician, clinical director), plus the patient portal. The front desk manages the schedule without seeing clinical content. Every record carries a company, so several locations can share one Odoo instance with isolated data.

Invoicing is a separate module

gh_dental_clinic_account, named Dental Clinic - Invoicing, connects dental records to Odoo Invoicing. It is optional and the clinical workflow does not depend on it. The details are in the guide to dental billing in Odoo.

Who it is for

  • Clinic owners already on Odoo who want scheduling, charts and billing without another system.
  • Odoo partners and integrators implementing Odoo for dental practices who need a module they can install and configure instead of building one.

It is not a clinical decision tool: dentists remain responsible for every clinical decision.

Requirements and price

Dental Clinic is built for Odoo 19 and licensed under OPL-1. Dental Clinic costs USD 349 on the Odoo Apps Store. Dental Clinic - Invoicing costs USD 448 and includes the base module.

Frequently asked questions

Is invoicing included in the Dental Clinic module?

No. Invoicing lives in a separate bridge module, Dental Clinic - Invoicing. The clinical module works on its own, and the bridge adds draft customer invoices from appointments, consultations and treatment plans.

Does the module send SMS reminders?

Only if you turn them on. SMS reminders are optional and disabled by default. When enabled, they use Odoo's standard SMS service, which consumes In-App Purchase credits bought separately from Odoo. Email confirmations and reminders do not need that service.

Does it give clinical advice or diagnoses?

No. It is a practice management tool. It records what the dentist enters and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment recommendations or clinical decision support.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes. The live demo is free, needs no registration and gives you a private sandbox with sample data for 120 minutes, in English or Spanish.
